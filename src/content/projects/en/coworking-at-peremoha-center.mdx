<TextContent>
  <Badge>The Goal</Badge>
  ## To create a space for working, studying, meetings, and events where people can work on their own projects, prepare for classes, or meet with their team and partners.
</TextContent>

<TextContent>
  <Badge>The Process</Badge>

Work on creating the coworking space began in August 2025. Together with volunteers from Repair Together, Chernihiv Events, and BUR Chernihiv, we dismantled the partitions, old ceiling, and projection equipment left over from the former cinema. Renovations continued with the support of Chernihiv Polytechnic National University. We completely replaced the electrical wiring and heating radiators, poured a finished floor, installed a new ceiling, and completed cosmetic renovations.

Special attention was paid to the details. We reused light fixtures from the "Peremoha" Center and preserved the windows of the former projection booth overlooking the small hall. Films used to be projected through them while projectionists monitored the screenings to ensure everything went according to plan. Now, the room behind the partition can serve as a backstage space where artists prepare for concerts and other events in the small hall.

</TextContent>

<TextContent>

The coworking space was created by Platform Ostriv in partnership with NGO "Eco Misto" and Chernihiv Polytechnic National University, with the support of the Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ). It is the first space implemented under the "Construction of Interaction: Shelter+" project and the eighth space created based on Platform Ostriv's methodologies.

</TextContent>

<TextContent>
  <Badge>The Approach</Badge>

The core principle of the "Shelter+" guidebook is to make the most efficient use of existing spaces and resources. Therefore, instead of a major structural overhaul, the teams from Platform Ostriv and NGO "Eco Misto" furnished the coworking space using DIY structures, furniture, and partitions. These elements make it possible to zone the space and easily adapt it for work, meetings, or events.

</TextContent>

<TextContent>

Every new space created under this approach also serves as an experiment. Structures, materials, and spatial organization methods are tested directly during implementation. Afterward, they are refined, standardized, and added to the "Shelter+" catalog. This gradually builds a system of open-source solutions that can be adapted to different spaces, needs, and cities.

For Chernihiv, the Ostriv team developed a new element—a translucent partition. It separates part of the room while letting light pass through and preserving the visual integrity of the space. Moving forward, this structure can be adapted for other shelters and added to the guidebook.

Another principle is to work with what is already available. Together with volunteers, we built the furniture for the coworking space right on-site during a workshop following instructions from the guidebook. Any community can create spaces like this on their own. For us, this is an important part of developing the maker community and culture.

</TextContent>
